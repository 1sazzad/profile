01 THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

A researcher at heart.
A maker by instinct.

I work at the intersection of computer vision, machine learning, and scientific discovery. My research journey has taken me from underwater imagery and model reliability to a growing focus on thermal infrared Earth observation.

I've completed the academic requirements for my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at the Institute of Science & Technology (IST), affiliated with National University, Bangladesh. My final degree results are pending.

Beyond research, I enjoy teaching, competitive programming, and building communities where people learn by doing.
